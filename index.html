Now enrolling · Phase III

Generalized myasthenia gravis (gMG)

A 9-month study of an investigational oral, once-daily treatment that blocks a specific immune-system protein linked to gMG muscle weakness. It is compared with a placebo over a 6-month treatment phase, followed by follow-up visits.

18–85age range
~9 mostudy length
~11clinic visits
1×/dayoral treatment

You may be eligible if you

  • Are between 18 and 85 years old
  • Have a confirmed gMG diagnosis with positive AChR antibodies
  • Have significant non-ocular symptoms, such as muscle weakness or fatigue
  • Have been on a stable gMG treatment for at least 6 months (for example NSISTs, IVIG/PLEX, FcRn antagonists or rituximab)

Study timeline

Treatment phase · 6 monthsFollow-up

Visit spacing is illustrative. Your coordinator gives you the exact schedule. A recent thymectomy (within 6 months), some recent treatments or an active infection may pause eligibility.

How enrollment works

Five steps, no surprises

We explain each step before it happens, and nothing study-related starts until you've signed the consent form.

1

Prescreen

Answer a few questions online in about a minute. Your information stays confidential.

2

Coordinator call

A study coordinator reviews your answers and medical history with you and answers your questions.

3

Informed consent

You learn the study's purpose, risks and benefits. Take your time, and bring family if you like.

4

Screening visit

Exams and tests at our Pasadena clinic confirm you meet the study criteria.

5

Study visits

Regular visits track your health and the treatment, then follow-up after the study ends.

      Questions

      Before you enroll

      What happens during enrollment?

      We explain the study in detail, you sign an informed consent form, and then you complete the screening tests and assessments that confirm you're eligible.

      How do I know if I qualify?

      Each study has its own criteria, such as age, diagnosis and current treatment. Start with the prescreen and our team will review your eligibility with you.

      Are clinical trials safe?

      Participant safety is our top priority. Every trial follows strict ethical guidelines and is approved and monitored by regulatory bodies and an Institutional Review Board (IRB).

      Will I be paid for taking part?

      Many studies offer compensation for your time and effort. The details vary by study and are explained before you enroll.

      Can I leave a study after joining?

      Yes. Participation is voluntary, and you can withdraw at any time for any reason without penalty.

      What is a clinical trial?

      A research study with volunteers that evaluates the safety, effectiveness and potential benefits of a new treatment, therapy or intervention.
